Tips for Your Dermatologist Visit

Prepare Your Questions

Write down your skin concerns, current routine, and any questions you have before your appointment.

Document Changes

Take photos of any skin changes or issues to show your dermatologist the progression over time.

Bring Your Products

Bring all skincare products and medications you're currently using for review.

Share Your History

Be prepared to discuss your family history of skin conditions and any previous treatments.
