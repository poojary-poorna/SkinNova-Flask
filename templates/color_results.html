{% if results.season == 'warm' %}

Warm Season Characteristics

You have golden, yellow, or peachy undertones that are enhanced by warm colors. Your natural beauty shines when you wear colors that echo the warmth in your complexion.

  • ✨ Golden or yellow undertones in skin
  • 🌟 Hair with warm tones (golden, auburn, warm brown)
  • 👁️ Eyes that are brown, amber, green, or warm hazel
  • 💍 Look best in gold jewelry
{% elif results.season == 'cool' %}

Cool Season Characteristics

You have pink, red, or blue undertones that are complemented by cool colors. Cool colors make your skin look clear and vibrant.

  • ✨ Pink, red, or blue undertones in skin
  • 🌟 Hair with cool tones (ash blonde, cool brown, black)
  • 👁️ Eyes that are blue, grey, green, or cool hazel
  • 💍 Look best in silver jewelry
{% else %}

Neutral Season Characteristics

You have balanced undertones that work beautifully with both warm and cool colors. You're versatile and can wear a wide range of colors successfully.

  • ✨ Balanced undertones - neither strongly warm nor cool
  • 🌟 Hair in neutral tones (medium brown, dark blonde)
  • 👁️ Eyes in soft, mixed tones
  • 💍 Look good in both gold and silver jewelry
{% endif %}

Wardrobe & Styling Guide

Professional Wardrobe

{% if results.season == 'warm' %}

Build your professional wardrobe around warm neutrals like cream, camel, and warm grey. Add pops of color with coral blazers or golden accessories.

Cream blazers Camel coats Warm grey suits Gold accessories
{% elif results.season == 'cool' %}

Your professional wardrobe shines with cool colors like navy, charcoal, and pure white. Add interest with jewel tones and silver details.

Navy suits Charcoal blazers Crisp white shirts Silver accessories
{% else %}

You have the flexibility to wear both warm and cool professional colors. Focus on muted, sophisticated tones for the most flattering look.

Soft grey suits Taupe blazers Off-white shirts Mixed metal accessories

Evening & Special Occasions

{% if results.season == 'warm' %}

Coral gowns Golden dresses Warm emerald Bronze details

Dusty rose Sage green Soft lavender Muted navy

Casual & Weekend

{% if results.season == 'warm' %}
